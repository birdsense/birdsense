Model Training

Fine-tune the classification model using confirmed and corrected data from the log

How it works

  1. Confirm or correct classifications in the Log to build training data
  2. Start training to fine-tune the pretrained ConvNeXt V2 model on your labeled images
  3. The model learns from your corrections — images are augmented (flipped, cropped, color-shifted) to prevent overfitting
  4. After training completes, click Reload Model to swap the running classifier to the fine-tuned version

Head-only trains just the final classification layer (fast, needs less data). Full fine-tunes all layers (slower, better with more data). The best model (lowest validation loss) is automatically saved.
